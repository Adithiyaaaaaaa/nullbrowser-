# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want NullBrowser to be a welcoming place for people learning, researching, and building privacy software. Be respectful, assume good faith, discuss ideas with evidence, and make room for contributors with different levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing another person's private information, or deliberately disrupting project spaces.

## Privacy-specific expectations

Do not post real browsing histories, authentication material, device identifiers, private URLs, or logs containing another person's data. Use synthetic examples and redact reports before sharing them.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the community. Reports should be sent privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Maintainers should protect the privacy of reporters whenever possible.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
